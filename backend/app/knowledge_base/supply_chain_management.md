# Supply Chain Management — Rules & SOPs

1. **Demand forecasting cadence.** Rolling 90-day demand forecast is refreshed
   weekly using consumption history and planned maintenance/turnaround
   schedules; turnaround-driven spare parts demand is modeled separately from
   routine consumption.
2. **Supplier performance scorecard.** Suppliers are scored monthly on
   on-time-delivery (OTD), quality reject rate, and responsiveness. OTD below
   85% for two consecutive months triggers a formal supplier review meeting.
3. **Lead-time SLA by category.** Standard MRO: 7 business days. Critical
   rotating-equipment spares: 3 business days expedited, with air-freight
   pre-authorization for single-source items. Bulk chemicals: per contract,
   typically 5-10 days with tanker/vessel scheduling dependency.
4. **Single-source risk mitigation.** Any material with only one approved
   supplier and no viable substitute is flagged high-risk; high-risk items
   carry elevated safety stock (2x standard buffer) and require an annual
   business-continuity review of that supplier.
5. **Logistics mode selection.** Air freight is authorized only for
   safety-critical or production-stoppage shortages where sea/road transit
   would exceed the operational impact window; all air-freight exceptions
   require Department Head sign-off given the cost premium.
6. **Customs and import compliance.** Imported materials require HS code
   classification and import permit validation before the purchase order is
   released to the supplier, to avoid port-of-entry delays.
7. **Vendor-managed inventory (VMI) rules.** VMI arrangements require the
   supplier to maintain agreed min/max stock levels on-site; company still
   performs independent cycle counts monthly to verify supplier compliance.
8. **Contract renewal lead time.** Strategic supply contracts are reviewed 90
   days before expiry (longer than the general Procurement 30-day window,
   given the sourcing lead time needed for critical categories).
9. **Disruption/force-majeure handling.** A supplier-declared force majeure
   triggers an immediate alternate-source evaluation and a risk memo to the
   Department Head within 24 hours, regardless of current stock position.
10. **Freight cost variance tolerance.** Actual freight invoice vs. quoted
    rate variance beyond 10% is routed to Supply Chain for carrier dispute
    before payment approval.
11. **Sustainability/ESG sourcing screen.** New strategic suppliers undergo an
    ESG screening questionnaire in addition to standard KYC; suppliers scoring
    below the minimum threshold require a documented risk-acceptance sign-off
    to proceed.
12. **Inventory optimization review.** Slow-moving stock (no consumption in 12
    months) is reviewed quarterly for redeployment across sites before
    write-off is considered.
