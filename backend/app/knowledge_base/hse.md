# HSE (Health, Safety & Environment) — Rules & SOPs

1. **Incident reporting timeline.** Any recordable incident (injury, near-miss
   with high potential, spill, fire) must be reported within 1 hour of
   occurrence via the mobile app or direct call to the HSE duty manager. A
   written follow-up report is due within 24 hours.
2. **Severity classification.** Incidents are classified Low / Medium / High /
   Critical. High and Critical severity auto-escalate to the HSE Manager and
   Site Director immediately, bypassing the normal review queue. Classification
   is based on actual outcome AND potential outcome (a near-miss with fatality
   potential is classified at the same severity as if it had occurred).
3. **PPE requirements by zone.** Process areas: hard hat, safety glasses, FR
   coveralls, steel-toe boots, mandatory at all times. H2S-monitored zones:
   personal H2S monitor required in addition to standard PPE. Confined space
   entry: full atmospheric testing (O2, LEL, H2S, CO) before entry and
   continuous monitoring during occupancy.
4. **Permit-to-work (PTW) rules.** Hot work, confined space entry, excavation,
   and work at height above 1.8m each require a distinct signed PTW valid for a
   single shift only. A PTW does not carry over to the next shift without
   re-inspection and re-signature by the incoming shift supervisor.
5. **Spill reporting thresholds.** Any spill reaching soil or water requires
   immediate containment and notification regardless of volume. Spills over 1
   barrel (159 L) trigger mandatory regulatory notification per environmental
   compliance procedure, with the volume estimate itself being an input to
   the incident record, not the sole trigger for whether to report.
6. **Certificate/compliance document validity.** Safety training certificates
   (H2S, confined space, working at height) are valid for 12 months; fire
   watch and first-aid certifications for 24 months. A certificate rejected for
   poor scan quality must be re-requested, not manually overridden.
7. **Compliance document follow-up cadence.** Missing-document requests are
   sent to the vendor/employee immediately, with reminders at day 3, day 7 and
   day 14. No response after 3 reminders (day 14) escalates to HSE Manager for
   access suspension consideration.
8. **Investigation workflow.** Every incident opens an investigation task
   assigned to the area supervisor. High/Critical incidents require a formal
   root-cause analysis (5-Why or Fishbone) completed within 5 business days.
9. **Environmental emissions thresholds.** Continuous emissions monitoring data
   is compared against regulatory permit limits at each reporting interval; any
   single reading exceeding the permit limit is an automatic non-conformance
   requiring regulator notification within the timeframe specified by the
   local permit (typically 24-72 hours).
10. **Equipment/location lookup failure handling.** If an incident references
    equipment or a location not found in the SAP Asset Master, the record is
    still created (safety reporting is never blocked on data quality) and
    flagged for asset-master correction separately.
11. **Lock-out/Tag-out (LOTO).** Mandatory for any work on equipment with
    stored energy (electrical, hydraulic, pneumatic, gravity). Each LOTO
    application/removal is logged with names of all personnel who applied
    locks.
12. **Monitoring station outage handling.** An offline monitoring station
    during a required reporting window is itself logged as a compliance
    exception; the last-known-good reading is not silently substituted.
